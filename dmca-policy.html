Legal / Copyright

DMCA Policy

Atomic Funnels respects intellectual property rights and responds promptly to valid copyright infringement claims. Below is how notices are submitted, reviewed, and resolved.

Last updated: December 3, 2024

How the process works

Four steps cover a claim from submission to resolution.

01

Submitting a Notice

If you believe your copyrighted work has been used without permission, send a written notice that includes:

  • •Physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or authorized representative
  • •Description of the copyrighted work claimed to be infringed
  • •Description of where the material is located on our site or service
  • •Your contact information, including address, phone number, and email
  • •A statement of good faith belief that the use is not authorized
  • •A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the owner

Send notices to

admin@atomicfunnels.com

Subject line: "DMCA Notice"

02

Our Response

Upon receiving a valid DMCA notice, we will:

  • •Remove or disable access to the claimed infringing material
  • •Notify the content provider of the removal
  • •Provide instructions for filing a counter-notice
  • •Document the incident on file

03

Counter-Notice Procedure

If content was removed due to a mistake or misidentification, the content provider may submit a counter-notice including:

  • •Physical or electronic signature
  • •Identification of the removed material and its prior location
  • •A statement, under penalty of perjury, of good faith belief that removal was a mistake
  • •Name, address, and phone number
  • •Consent to the jurisdiction of the applicable federal court

04

Repeat Infringers

We maintain a repeat infringer policy. Multiple valid infringement claims against the same content provider will result in account termination. We reserve the right to terminate an account for a single infringement in appropriate circumstances.

We reserve the right to update this policy at any time. Continued use of our services constitutes acceptance of any changes. We respect fair use principles under copyright law and make fair use determinations on a case-by-case basis. This policy provides information about our DMCA compliance and is not legal advice; consult an attorney for legal guidance specific to your situation.
