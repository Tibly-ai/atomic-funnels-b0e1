Policy

We provide professional development and design services for marketing funnels. Our payment is for the labor hours invested in creating a custom system for your business, not a guarantee of profitability. We employ proven scripting frameworks, compelling copy strategies, and rigorous split testing of CTAs and customer journeys to maximize your chances of a positive return, but success depends on factors outside our control.

Delivery Estimate

All sales are final. Here is exactly what that covers.

Payment confirms your understanding and acceptance that no refunds will be issued once services have commenced. Below is the full breakdown of what we deliver, what we cannot guarantee, and where responsibility sits on each side.

Payment For Labor

Fees cover expertise, not outcomes

The fees you pay are for our expertise and the labor hours required to design, write, and build the agreed-upon funnel assets, not for a specific business result.

Deliverables Guarantee

3 to 4 week delivery window

We commit to delivering the specified funnel assets within an estimated timeframe of 3 to 4 weeks. Should a project run longer, that delay is not grounds for a refund.

Service Scope

What is included in every build

  • Production of all funnel assets outlined in your service agreement
  • Completion within the estimated 3 to 4 week period
  • The agreed number of revision rounds
  • Split-test asset variations built to optimize performance

Non-Guaranteed Outcomes

What we cannot promise

  • The overall profitability or performance of your business
  • A positive return on ad spend or specific financial outcomes
  • Marketing performance tied to any particular advertising strategy

Client Responsibilities

What stays on your side

  • Ensuring your business and marketing practices comply with relevant laws
  • Actively implementing the marketing strategy we design and deliver

Outside Our Control

Factors that affect results

  • CPMs and ad platform choice
  • Product/market fit and audience selection
  • Ad spend budget and ad management partners

Our role is to provide the deliverables and strategic frameworks to strengthen your marketing efforts. External factors and client-specific actions ultimately determine your business outcomes, and the success of your business remains your responsibility.

Questions about this policy before you start?

Read through the scope of your project agreement, then reach out. We are glad to walk through what is included, what the revision rounds cover, and how the split-test assets are structured before you commit.
