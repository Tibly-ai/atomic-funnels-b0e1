What this policy covers

Six areas govern how we handle information on our site and through our forms. Read the section that matters to you, or all of it.

01

Data Collection and Usage

When you use our site, we or our authorized technology service providers may collect technical and routing information to facilitate your use of the site and its services. This helps us administer the site, understand and measure traffic patterns, identify favorite areas of our users, determine areas needing improvement, understand technological requirements, and improve our site for mobile and future technical platforms.

02

Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies during your visit to function as your identification card. This lets us record passwords and preferences, track purchases, and deliver targeted messages or advertising. Most browsers initially accept cookies, and you can set yours to notify you when a cookie arrives, giving you the choice to accept it.

03

Opt-Out Options

To opt out of targeted advertising, contact us at admin@atomicfunnels.com. You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the unsubscribe link at the bottom of each message.

04

Data Storage and Security

We only store information you submit through our forms. Sensitive information, including credit card data, is encrypted. Other information is stored via cookies or pixels as described above.

05

We will release specific information to comply with valid legal inquiries or processes, including search warrants, subpoenas, statutes, and court orders. These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Wyoming, and any legal actions are subject to mediation in Wyoming.

06

Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this privacy policy at any time. Continued use of our site after a change constitutes your acceptance of that change. Check back periodically for updates.
